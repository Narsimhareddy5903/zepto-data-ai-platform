Query 5 - JOIN
======================================================================
SQL:
SELECT
            b.title,
            b.price_gbp,
            b.rating,
            c.category_name
        FROM books AS b
        JOIN categories AS c
            ON b.category_id = c.category_id
        ORDER BY b.price_gbp DESC
        LIMIT 10;
